📚 WHAT DO THESE METRICS MEAN? (SYSTEM EVALUATION GUIDE)

🎯 Precision & Recall

Precision: Percentage of reports flagged as high priority that are genuine operational emergencies.
Recall: Percentage of actual ground truth emergencies successfully surfaced to emergency operators.

⚡ Confidence Cap & Urgency

Confidence Cap: Caps evidence score at 65% when media/source verification is lacking.
Priority Boost: Ensures life-safety threats reach emergency operators immediately even with unverified sources.

⏳ Freshness Decay & Conflicts

Freshness Decay: Reduces priority by 15% every 2 hours unless renewed by fresh reports.
Conflict Detection: Flags contradictory reports (e.g. flooded vs clear) for human review.

📈 Precision, Recall & Surfaced Reports vs Threshold

Role-Specific Recommended Thresholds

0.30 Threshold (Responder / High Recall):

Surfaces ~90% of reports. Ensures zero missed life-safety threats. Ideal for search & rescue field teams.

0.50 Threshold (Balanced Default):

Optimal balance between high precision (~82%) and strong recall (~78%). Standard for emergency dispatch.

0.65 Threshold (City Official / High Precision):

Filters out noise (~92% precision). Focuses strictly on verified high-confidence reports for public announcements.
