🏛️ 1. Problem Statement & System Architecture

During major municipal disasters, emergency command centers face an overwhelming influx of unverified crowd reports across multiple channels. The Disaster Response Verification Engine provides a transparent, rule-based decision-support system that processes crowd reports, evaluates evidence quality, quantifies operational priority, flags conflicting claims, and assists human emergency dispatchers.

Disaster Reports (CSV) → Evidence Processing → Confidence Engine → Freshness Engine → Priority Engine → Conflict Detection → Duplicate Clustering → Triage Queue → Human Verification
🛡️ 2. Confidence Engine Formula

The Confidence Score estimates how trustworthy a report is based on verifiable evidence (0–100 scale). It combines six transparently weighted criteria:

Confidence = 0.15 × SourceReliability + 0.25 × Corroboration + 0.15 × Freshness
             + 0.10 × LocationPrecision + 0.15 × MediaEvidence + 0.20 × ResponderVerification

Corroboration 25% 0 independent reports (0.00), 1–2 (0.50), 3–4 (0.75), 5+ (1.00)

🔒 3. Confidence Cap Rule

To prevent single unverified rumors from surfacing as High or Very High confidence based on recency alone, a mandatory confidence cap is enforced:

IF Source = "Citizen Report" AND Independent Corroboration = 0 AND Media = None/Unavailable AND Verification = Pending/Needs Review
THEN Confidence Score is CAPPED at 69.0 (Medium Category).

Note: Missing media or lack of corroboration is NOT treated as proof that a report is false. It simply indicates that human verification is recommended.

⚡ 4. Priority Score Engine & Urgency Triage

Priority answers: "Which incident should emergency responders inspect first?" Priority is distinct from Confidence:

Priority = 0.35 × IncidentSeverity + 0.30 × ConfidenceScore + 0.20 × FreshnessUrgency + 0.15 × OperationalUrgency
💡
KEY PRINCIPLE: CONFIDENCE ≠ PRIORITY
Reports concerning severe hazards (e.g., Evacuation Needed or Medical Emergency) receive high priority scores even if confidence is initially low. This ensures that unverified high-risk life-safety incidents are immediately flagged for human verification rather than ignored.
⏳ 5. Freshness Engine Rules

Freshness tracks the temporal decay of crowd observation relevance:

Stale reports remain visible in all views and are never hidden automatically.

⚔️ 6. Conflict & Duplicate Clustering Engine

Duplicate Clustering: Near-identical observations occurring in close spatial proximity (≤ 0.5 km) and time window (≤ 3 hours) are grouped into a correlation cluster. Correlated reports are discounted when calculating independent corroboration to prevent multiple duplicate reports from artificially inflating confidence.

Conflict Detection: Proximal reports claiming opposing operational conditions (e.g. Road completely blocked vs Road passable) or conflicting verification statuses are flagged neutrally with: "Conflicting observations detected. Human verification recommended."

👨‍🚒 7. Human Verification & Operational Roles

The system operates strictly as a human-in-the-loop decision-support tool. No automated enforcement, permit rejection, or emergency vehicle deployment is ever executed automatically.

🚨 Responder Role

Emphasizes high recall, fresh reports, and the urgent Priority Queue for fast initial triage.

🏛️ City Official Role

Emphasizes high precision, verified evidence quality, and departmental oversight.

⚖️ Coordinator Role

Emphasizes conflict resolution, verification gaps, and citywide resource allocation.

🔒 8. Privacy Guarantees & System Governance

The Disaster Response Command System strictly adheres to the following privacy and ethical governance rules:

  • ❌ No Citizen Reputation Scores: All citizen reports use the exact same baseline source reliability (0.40).
  • ❌ No Individual Profiling: No user tracking, behavioral profiling, or personal identity scoring.
  • ❌ No IP Tracking or Device Fingerprinting: Duplicate detection relies solely on spatial and temporal report coordinates.
  • ❌ No Continuous Tracking: Citizens are never continuously tracked or monitored.
  • ❌ No Automatic Enforcement: All verification and resource dispatch decisions remain 100% human-authorized.
