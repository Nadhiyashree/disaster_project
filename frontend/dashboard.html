OPERATIONAL CONCEPT: CONFIDENCE ≠ PRIORITY
Confidence estimates how trustworthy the report's evidence is (0–100). Priority estimates how urgently responders should inspect the report. A high-severity incident (such as Evacuation Needed or Medical Emergency) with low initial confidence will still surface near the top of the queue for human verification.
